03 / REPRODUCIBILITY
An explicit set of assumptions
Exact means exact
IUPAC motif letters expand to sets of concrete DNA bases. Ambiguous sequence bases never match, even motif N. Overlaps are counted. Reverse-strand coordinates refer to the original forward sequence. Reverse-complement palindromes count once, with strand “both”; other motifs can count at the same position on both strands.
Conditioned on composition
Mononucleotide treats concrete positions as exchangeable within each record. Dinucleotide conditions additionally on each concrete run’s endpoints and adjacent-pair counts. Short runs and degenerate spaces may never change. Neither accounts for arbitrary repeats, regional composition, or longer-range dependence. Filters change the match view only; they do not recalculate statistics.
